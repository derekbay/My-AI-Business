The Process

How AI Video Creation Works

From brief to final video in five straightforward steps — no technical knowledge or creative background required from you.

1
Discovery Call

We learn about your business, the service or message you want to promote, your target audience, and where the video will be used. We also discuss your brand style and the tone you want to strike.

2
Script & Brief

We write the video script and share it with you for approval before any production begins. You can refine the wording until the message is exactly right. Once approved, we lock in the brief and quote.

3
AI Production

We produce the video using AI tools — generating visuals, applying your branding, adding voiceover, captions, and music. Most videos are ready for review within 3-5 business days.

4
Review & Revise

You watch the draft and give feedback. Two rounds of revisions are included so you can adjust visuals, timing, voiceover pacing, or any other element until the video is exactly what you wanted.

5
Delivery

You receive the final video in all required formats — ready to upload to your website, social media, or ad campaigns. Full ownership of the files is transferred to you on delivery.
