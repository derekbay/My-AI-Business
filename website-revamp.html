The Process

How a Website Revamp Works

Five clear steps from first call to live site — you know exactly what's happening at every stage.

1
Free Discovery Call

We learn about your business, your customers, and what you want your website to achieve. No pitch — just the right questions so the solution we design actually solves the right problem.

2
Audit & Brief

We review your existing site (if any), agree on scope, page structure, and the content needed. You receive a written proposal with a fixed price before any work begins.

3
Design & Build

We design and build your new site with regular check-ins so nothing surprises you. You see real progress — not a black-box process that ends with a big reveal.

4
Review & Revise

You review every page and we refine until it is exactly right. Two rounds of revisions are included — so your feedback genuinely shapes the final product.

5
Launch & Handover

We go live together and hand over full ownership — all files, passwords, and documentation. Your website belongs entirely to you from day one.
