You Might Need an AI Caller If Any of These Sound Familiar

Leads enquire but go cold before you get a chance to call

Speed-to-lead is everything — research shows the odds of qualifying a lead drop dramatically after the first five minutes. An AI caller follows up within seconds of an enquiry coming in, before interest fades.

You spend too much time making routine follow-up calls

If a significant part of your day goes to calling leads, confirming appointments, or chasing bookings, that is time you could be spending on actual client work. AI handles the repetitive calls so you don't have to.

Automated reminder calls the day before and the morning of an appointment consistently reduce no-shows. An AI caller handles this across your entire appointment book without any effort on your part.

The Process

How the AI Caller Works

Five steps from discovery to a live AI caller making calls on your behalf — set up once, works continuously.

1
Discovery Call

We learn about your business, the types of calls you want the AI to make, your target contacts, and the outcomes you want from each call. We map out exactly what the AI should say and do at each stage of the conversation.

2
Script & Call Flow Design

We write a detailed call script and conversation flow covering the opening, key messages, responses to common questions or objections, booking steps, and fallback for complex situations. You review and approve everything before build begins.

3
Build & Configuration

We build and configure the AI voice agent — selecting and tuning the voice, setting up call triggers, connecting to your calendar or CRM if needed, and ensuring compliance with relevant phone regulations in your region.

4
Test Calls & Refinement

We run test calls so you can hear exactly how the AI sounds and responds to different scenarios. You give feedback and we refine the voice, pacing, and script until everything sounds and works exactly right.

5
Go Live & Monitor

The AI caller goes live and begins making calls according to your schedule and triggers. We monitor early performance, review call logs, and make any adjustments needed to optimise results. You receive a handover so you can manage the system going forward.
