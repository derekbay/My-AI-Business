Is This For You?

You Might Need SEO & AEO If Any of These Sound Familiar

You Google your own business and can't find it

If your potential customers search for your services locally or nationally and you don't appear, you are effectively invisible, and your competitors are taking that traffic.

Your business isn't being mentioned by AI search engines

More and more people ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews for business recommendations. If you're not optimised for AEO, you're missing this fast-growing discovery channel entirely.

You rely entirely on referrals or paid ads for new customers

Referrals dry up and ads cost money every month. SEO creates a compounding asset, content that keeps driving traffic and leads long after it's published, without ongoing ad spend.

Your site gets traffic but visitors don't convert

If people find you but don't enquire, the problem is often mismatched content, you're ranking for the wrong keywords. A proper SEO strategy aligns your content with buyer intent.
