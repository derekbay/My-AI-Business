Is This For You?

You Might Need an AI Chatbot If Any of These Sound Familiar

You miss enquiries that come in after hours

Most website visitors don't come back a second time. If they land outside business hours and can't get an answer, they move on to a competitor who responds immediately.

You answer the same questions over and over

If you spend hours each week answering the same pricing questions, FAQs, and service queries, an AI chatbot can handle all of these automatically — freeing your time for higher-value work.

Your contact form gets lots of low-quality enquiries

A chatbot can qualify leads before they reach you — asking the right questions to filter out tyre-kickers and make sure only serious, relevant enquiries land in your inbox.

You want to convert more visitors without spending more on ads

Increasing the conversion rate of your existing traffic is far more cost-effective than driving more traffic. A chatbot that engages visitors at the right moment can significantly increase the percentage who enquire.

The Process

How AI Chatbot Integration Works

From discovery call to a live chatbot on your website — a clear, straightforward process with no technical knowledge required from you.

1
Free Discovery Call

We learn about your business, your most common customer questions, the leads you want to capture, and how you would like the chatbot to handle different enquiry types.

2
Knowledge & Scope

You share your services, FAQs, pricing, and any other information the chatbot needs to know. We turn this into a structured knowledge base and send you a written proposal with a fixed price before any work begins.

3
Build & Train

We build and train the chatbot on your business, customise its appearance to match your brand, and configure lead capture and any booking flows. You see a live preview before anything goes near your website.

4
Test & Refine

You test the chatbot and we refine its responses until it sounds and behaves exactly as you want. We run through edge cases, difficult questions, and off-topic inputs to make sure it handles everything gracefully.

5
Launch & Handover

We install the chatbot on your website with a single code snippet — typically a five-minute job. We walk you through how to update its knowledge as your business changes and hand over full ownership.
